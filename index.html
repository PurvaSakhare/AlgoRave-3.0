About

01

ABOUT THE EVENT

The Paper Presentation is an engaging academic event designed to encourage students to explore, research and present innovative ideas on current and emerging technology-related topics.

The event provides a platform to develop technical knowledge, research skills, communication skills, confidence and the ability to present ideas clearly and effectively.

Participants will present a technical paper before a judging panel within a fixed time limit.

RULES & REGULATIONS

01

Each paper may be presented by a maximum of 3–4 participants.

02

Participants must complete the official registration process before the specified deadline.

03

The paper must be based on a technical, scientific, digital, AI, computing, cybersecurity, or other technology-related topic.

04

The paper title/topic and abstract must be submitted according to the instructions provided by the organizers.

05

The presentation must be completed within the allotted time, normally 8–10 minutes.

06

Participants should clearly explain the problem statement, objectives, methodology, key findings/ideas, and conclusion.

07

Participants may use factual examples, statistics, diagrams, technical concepts, and real-world applications to support their presentation.

08

The presentation must be original and technically accurate. Plagiarism, deliberate misinformation, or misrepresentation of technical facts may lead to disqualification.

09

Participants must answer questions from the judging panel during the Q&A session.

10

Personal remarks, offensive language, discrimination, disrespectful behaviour, or inappropriate content are strictly prohibited.

11

The use of mobile phones, internet sources, or other external aids during the presentation will be permitted only if explicitly allowed by the organizers.

12

Participants must report to the venue before the assigned reporting time.

13

Late arrival may result in disqualification or denial of participation, at the discretion of the organizers.

14

Participants must follow all instructions given by the event coordinators and judging panel.

15

The decision of the judging panel shall be final and binding.
